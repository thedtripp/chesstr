Sound effects from the "sfx" pack in [lichess-org/lila](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila)
(`public/sound/sfx/`), one of the few sound packs in that repo actually covered
by an open license rather than lichess's default all-rights-reserved sound set
(see `COPYING.md` in that repo).

- Author: [Enigmahack](https://github.com/Enigmahack)
- License: AGPLv3+
- `Confirmation.mp3` here is `GenericNotify.mp3` in the source pack (the
  original `Confirmation.mp3` is a symlink to it).
